Community, Accessibility, Creation

Formed through collaboration and friendships, we are a Chicago-based art collective that centers on sustainable practices and accessible skill-sharing. Composed of artists (Members names here?) driven by a desire to imagine alternative approaches to materials, we work with creative reuse, alternative photography, and/or natural materials. Encouraging our diverse skillsets to inform each other through collaboration and skill sharing, we create dynamic assemblages, and host outdoor art shows in our Living Lot (a garden lot attached to one of our members' apartments). Exploring spaces outside of traditional galleries to develop an accessible space to show work in community. Further expanding our mode of collaboration by hosting workshops that provide participants with the knowledge to create sustainable functional art objects. Leveraging our skills to impart new ways of engaging with materials to others in practical ways. Our workshops are designed to build on one another and create environments for approachable learning. Connecting the artists is a shared interest in the space between the body and technology, motivated by the question of what collaboration using systems like analog/digital media.
